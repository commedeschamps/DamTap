# Prospective Stakeholder Interview Guide — NOT YET ADMINISTERED

**Status:** Future research instrument. No responses or participants are claimed.

## Consent introduction

"We are Software Engineering students researching requirements for the DamTap diploma project. Participation is voluntary; responses will be summarized without your name unless you explicitly consent otherwise. You may skip questions or stop. May we take anonymous notes?" Seek separate permission for any recording.

## Business representatives

1. Which food products remain unsold most often, and when can you estimate the quantity?
2. What currently happens to eligible unsold food?
3. Do you already offer discounts near closing time? How are they set?
4. What minimum selling price and discount schedule would you accept?
5. Would automatic downward markdowns be acceptable if you could stop them?
6. What information must customers see about safety, ingredients and pickup?
7. How would staff prepare, identify and hand over reserved portions?
8. What would prevent you from participating?

## Potential customers

1. Would you consider collecting discounted surplus food on the same day?
2. Which matters most: discount, venue, food type, collection time or distance?
3. What details would you require to trust an offer?
4. How long would you travel for pickup?
5. Would a price that decreases near closing time be clear and acceptable?
6. What reservation confirmation and cancellation options would you expect?
7. What concerns would make you avoid such an offer?

## Observation template

Venue code (not name without consent); observation date/time; category and approximate eligible surplus; moment surplus becomes known; existing pricing method; latest permitted sale time; packaging/pickup steps; existing inventory system; staff burden; consent record.

After collection, connect each genuine finding to a requirement ID and note disagreements and negative feedback.
